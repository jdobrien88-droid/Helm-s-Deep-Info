Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

My Orchestrator is a personal, single-user home automation and information dashboard. It is not a public product, is not distributed to other users, and has exactly one user: its owner.

What data this app accesses

With the owner's own Google account sign-in, this app requests read-only access to:

  • Google Calendar (view events)
  • Gmail (read messages)
  • Google Drive (view files)
  • Google Tasks (view tasks)

How that data is used

Data retrieved from these Google APIs is used only to display the owner's own information back to them inside their private dashboard, which runs on hardware the owner personally controls. Examples: showing upcoming calendar events, surfacing relevant emails, or referencing files when the owner asks the dashboard a question.

What this app does not do

  • It does not share, sell, or rent any Google data to third parties.
  • It does not use Google data for advertising or marketing of any kind.
  • It does not allow any other person to view the owner's Google data.
  • It does not write to, modify, or delete anything in the owner's Google account; access is read-only.

Data storage

OAuth tokens and any cached data from Google APIs are stored locally on hardware the owner controls and are not transmitted to any external server other than Google's own APIs.

Revoking access

The owner can revoke this app's access at any time from their Google Account's third-party access settings.
