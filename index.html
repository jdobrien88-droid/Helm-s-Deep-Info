My Orchestrator

This is a personal, single-user home automation and information dashboard. It is not a public product, is not available for sign-up, and is used only by its owner to view their own calendar, tasks, and personal records in one place.

Google Account Access

This app connects to the owner's own Google account using read-only access to Calendar, Gmail, Drive, and Tasks, solely so the owner can view their own schedule and information inside their personal dashboard. No data is shared with any third party, sold, or used for advertising. No other person's data is accessed by this app.

Privacy

Data retrieved from Google APIs is used only to display information back to the account owner within their own private dashboard, which runs on hardware the owner controls. It is not transmitted to any other service.
